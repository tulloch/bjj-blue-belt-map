Blue Belt Map

From any position — what's your next move?

Top · Dominant

Mount Stay heavy. Take the back if they turn. Back Mount Strangle is the priority. RNC, bow & arrow. Side Control Kimura grip is gold. Hunt the far arm. Knee on Belly Pressure for tap or transition. They scramble. North-South Heavy chest. Isolate arms. Bridge to side.

Top · Passing & Neutral

Half Guard Top Free the leg, pass to side. Or submit from here. Closed Guard Top Posture. Open. Pass. Don't get triangled. Open Guard Top (Passing) Pick a pass that fits their guard.

Bottom · Active

Closed Guard Bottom Highest-leverage bottom position. Sweep + sub. Open Guard Bottom Spider, DLR, lasso, butterfly. Distance + sweep. Half Guard Bottom Get the underhook. Sweep to top.

Bottom · Escape

Side Control Bottom Don't accept staying here. Recover guard. Mount Bottom Worst pin. Upa or elbow-knee escape. Back Mount Bottom Defend the choke first. Then escape. Turtle Don't stay here. Roll, sit-out, recover.

How to use this

  1. Open the position you're stuck in.
  2. Pick the section that matches what your training partner is doing — "If they bridge", "If they shrimp", etc.
  3. Tap a move to open a YouTube search. Pick a video that fits your style.
  4. If you lose the position, the bottom of every page tells you where you'll end up — go to that page next.
Note — This is a decision tree, not a move catalogue. Each option is keyed to what your opponent is doing, because that's the blue-belt skill: reading reactions and picking the right counter without freezing.
