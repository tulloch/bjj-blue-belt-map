Mount

📷 See this position
Goal here Stay heavy, isolate an arm or expose the neck. If they turn belly-down, take the back — don't fight to keep mount.

If they react…

🌉 They bridge & roll (upa)
🟡 Step over to S-Mount
Kill the bridge by climbing high — sets up armbar.
▶
🟡 Switch to Technical Mount
As they roll to the side, follow the hip — hunt the back.
▶
🟢 Take the Back →
If they fully expose, slide the hooks in.
→
🦐 They shrimp / elbow-knee escape
🟡 Re-mount with knee shield
Drop the knee back over before they recover guard.
▶
🟢 Armbar the far arm
As they push, isolate the post arm and spin.
▶
🟢 Mounted triangle
If their arm slides across, trap the head + arm. Long legs make this your cheat code.
▶
🔄 They turn belly-down
🟢 Take the Back →
Highest-percentage option. Hooks in, seatbelt grip.
→
🟢 Bow & arrow choke setup
Once you have the back — collar grip, far leg trap. Lachlan Giles walkthrough.
▶
🛡️ They frame on your hips
🟡 Ezekiel choke
Their frames create the gap — feed your sleeve.
▶
🟡 Arm triangle (head & arm)
Pin one of their framing arms across their neck. Compress-heavy — easier with mass.
▶
🟡 Gift wrap their arm
Pull their wrist across their face — opens chokes + back.
▶
💪 They bench-press / push your chest
🟡 Climb to high mount + grapevine legs
Kills the bench press — flatten their hips. Harder at 66kg, but doable.
▶
🟡 Americana on the post arm
Their own push gives you the angle to paint the floor.
▶
