Back Mount Bottom

📷 See this position
Goal here · BLUE-BELT PRIORITY Defend the choke first — survival comes before escape. Pin their choking hand, fight their grips, then escape out the bottom.

Order of operations

🛡️ 1 — Defend the choke (always first)
🟢 Pin their choking hand to your chest
Both your hands on their wrist — chin tucked.
▶
🟢 Hand-fight the over-arm
Strip the high seatbelt before it locks in.
▶
⬇️ 2 — Scoot down (the escape)
🟢 Slide down past the hooks
Sit your weight on their hip, scoot toward their feet.
▶
🟢 Strip the second hook
First hook out, then the second — don't let them re-hook.
▶
↪️ 3 — Roll to the choke side
🟢 Roll toward their over-arm shoulder
Strong-side roll — limits their choke leverage.
▶
🟡 Land in their guard or side control
Worst case is side control bottom — still better than back.
▶

Things they'll try — defend

⚠️ Bow & arrow choke (gi)
🟢 Hand on their grip + turn into them
Their lapel grip = death. Strip + roll into the choke side.
▶
⚠️ Body triangle
🟢 Walk the lock down to your knees
Body triangle = harder to scoot. Drop the lock past hips first.
▶

Where you're going

If your escape works:

Mount Bottom (still escaping) Side Control Bottom Turtle Closed Guard Top (best case)
