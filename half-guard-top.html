Half Guard Top

📷 See this position
Goal here Cross-face hard, kill the underhook, free the trapped leg. Pass to side control or mount. Don't let them get the underhook or deep half.

If they react…

🤝 They get the underhook
Whizzer + base out
Don't let them sweep — overhook their underhook arm.
▶
Kimura the underhook arm
Their elbow exposed — figure-four and pull through.
▶
Knee-slide pass anyway
Underhook isn't fatal if you pass fast.
▶
🤿 They go to deep half guard
Defend deep half — base wide
Sit high, post on their head, free the leg.
▶
Kimura grip counter
Trap their far arm before they can sweep.
▶
🔒 They hit the lockdown
Patience + walk it out
Don't fight the lockdown — angle out and free the leg.
▶
Defend the electric chair
Posture up, kill the lapel grip before they invert.
▶
🛡️ They get a knee shield (reverse half)
Leg drag pass
Drag the shield knee across, pin to the floor.
▶
Knee cut pass
Slice your knee through their hip line.
▶
Smash pass
Drive shoulder across, flatten their hips.
