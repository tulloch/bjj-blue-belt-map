Knee on Belly

📷 See this position
Goal here Pressure for the tap or capitalise on their scramble. Don't camp here — they're moving, so you should be moving too. Far-arm grip + collar grip in gi.

If they react…

👋 They push your knee with both hands
Far-side armbar
Their pushing arm is straight — spin and finish.
▶
Near-side armbar
Step over the head while they're committed to pushing.
▶
↩️ They turn away from you
Take the Back →
Free gift. Hooks in, seatbelt grip.
→
Slide to Mount →
If they're flat, ride the turn into mount.
→
✊ They reach for grips on you
Baseball bat choke
Cross-grip their collars, twist down.
▶
Lapel choke (gi)
Feed your own lapel across.
▶
🔄 They roll / explode out
Follow to Back →
If the roll exposes the back, take it.
→
Drop back to Side Control →
If you're losing it, settle into side rather than guard.
→
