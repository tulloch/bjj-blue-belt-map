Section 1 — Blue belt = ESCAPES (drill these first)

Per Saulo Ribeiro: white belt = survival, blue belt = escapes. For you specifically — light frame, can't out-strength anyone — escapes are the foundation that keeps you on the mat.

🟢 Upa (bridge & roll) from mount Kesting 🟢 Elbow-knee escape from mount Gordon Ryan 🟢 Bridge & shrimp from side control ESG 🟢 Knee shield to half guard universal 🟢 Defend the choke first (back escape) Kesting 🟢 Sit-out from turtle wrestling fundamental

Section 2 — Your top 5 submissions (after escapes are solid)

  1. Cross-collar choke — from mount and from closed guard. Roger Gracie's signature, pure Carlson lineage.
  2. Triangle — from closed guard, mount, or back. Long legs make this a cheat code for you.
  3. Armbar — closed guard, mount, back. Long arms = stronger extension.
  4. Kimura — half guard top, side control, north-south, closed guard. Your long arms make the figure-four lock devastating.
  5. Bow & arrow choke — from back. Leverage-based, gi-specific, ages well. Carlson staple.

Section 4 — Carlson context (read once)

What your coach will drill on you

  1. Pressure passing. Carlson is a "warrior style" school — heavy top pressure, smash passing, knee-on-belly. You won't be the one finishing these passes (66kg can't), but you NEED to know them to defend them. They're yellow on your map, not grey.
  2. Takedowns + cross-training. Carlson curriculum includes Judo / wrestling. Know the basics, but feel free to pull guard in rolls — you don't owe anyone a takedown at 50.
  3. The "old school 5" submissions. Cross-collar, kimura, americana, armbar, triangle. All on your green list. Drill them.
  4. Mario Sperry Master Series is the canonical Carlson guard-passing reference. Watch Closed Guard Passing, Open Guard Passing, Half Guard Passing to understand what your coach is teaching.

Section 6 — Off-mat work

The four non-negotiables at 50

  1. Strength + mass. At 182cm/66kg, putting on 5–8kg of muscle would massively reduce injury risk and add submission strength without sacrificing leverage. Goblet squats, dead hangs, kettlebell swings, push-ups. 2× a week.
  2. Mobility. Hips and shoulders. 10 min daily. Non-negotiable.
  3. Cardio. Zone-2 (HR 120–140) on off-days. Matters more for you than picking up a new sweep.
  4. Tap early. Ego costs you weeks. The roll matters less than the next 100 rolls.
