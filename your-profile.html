Section 1 — Blue belt = ESCAPES (drill these first)

Per Saulo Ribeiro: white belt = survival, blue belt = escapes. For you specifically — light frame, can't out-strength anyone — escapes are the foundation that keeps you on the mat.

🟢 Upa (bridge & roll) from mount Kesting 🟢 Elbow-knee escape from mount Gordon Ryan 🟢 Bridge & shrimp from side control ESG 🟢 Knee shield to half guard universal 🟢 Defend the choke first (back escape) Kesting 🟢 Sit-out from turtle wrestling fundamental

Section 2 — Your top 5 submissions (after escapes are solid)

  1. Cross-collar choke — from mount and from closed guard. Roger Gracie's signature, pure Carlson lineage.
  2. Triangle — from closed guard, mount, or back. Long legs make this a cheat code for you.
  3. Armbar — closed guard, mount, back. Long arms = stronger extension.
  4. Kimura — half guard top, side control, north-south, closed guard. Your long arms make the figure-four lock devastating.
  5. Bow & arrow choke — from back. Leverage-based, gi-specific, ages well. Carlson staple.

Section 3 — Sweeps & passes for your build

🟢 Hip bump sweep + kimura combo Lachlan Giles 🟢 Sit-up sweep from closed guard Lucas Lepri 🟢 Spider / lasso / DLR sweeps long-limb game 🟢 Knee cut pass (technique-based) Lucas Lepri 🟢 Leg drag pass universal

Section 4 — Carlson context (read once)

What your coach will drill on you

  1. Pressure passing. Carlson is a "warrior style" school — heavy top pressure, smash passing, knee-on-belly. You won't be the one finishing these passes (66kg can't), but you NEED to know them to defend them. They're yellow on your map, not grey.
  2. Takedowns + cross-training. Carlson curriculum includes Judo / wrestling. Know the basics, but feel free to pull guard in rolls — you don't owe anyone a takedown at 50.
  3. The "old school 5" submissions. Cross-collar, kimura, americana, armbar, triangle. All on your green list. Drill them.
  4. Mario Sperry Master Series is the canonical Carlson guard-passing reference. Watch Closed Guard Passing, Open Guard Passing, Half Guard Passing to understand what your coach is teaching.

Section 6 — Off-mat work (the program)

At 50, recovery is the constraint, not motivation. Don't try to do it all — pick what you can sustain. Sleep + protein + 2× strength + daily mobility is a complete week. Add cardio on off-days when you can.

💪 1. Strength + mass (2× per week, ~40 min)

Goal: Add 5–8kg of lean muscle over 6–12 months. This is the single highest-ROI off-mat work for you. Light + lean + ageing = injury risk; muscle is armour. Plus submissions get more reliable as you stop being the lightest person on the mat by 20kg.

Day A — Lower + pull

  • Goblet squat — 3 sets × 8 reps
  • Romanian deadlift (DB or KB) — 3 × 8
  • Pull-ups or chin-ups (banded if needed) — 3 × AMRAP
  • Dead hang — 3 × 30 sec
  • Hollow hold — 3 × 30 sec

Day B — Upper + carry

  • Push-ups or bench press — 3 × 10
  • Dumbbell row — 3 × 10/side
  • Kettlebell swing — 3 × 15
  • Turkish get-up — 3 × 3/side
  • Farmer's carry (heavy DBs) — 3 × 30m
  • Plank — 3 × 45 sec

Why these specifically: compound lifts (whole body in 40 min), grip-heavy (gi transfer), bridge / get-up patterns (mat-specific), joint-friendly. No max deadlifts, no heavy bench, no CrossFit MetCons. You're not training to compete in lifting — you're armouring up for grappling.

Progression: add 1–2.5kg every 2 weeks until form breaks. Then hold there.

🧘 2. Mobility (10 min daily — non-negotiable)

Goal: Hips, shoulders, T-spine, ankles, wrists. All five get hammered in BJJ — all five lose range with age unless you train them. 10 minutes daily > 60 minutes weekly.

Daily 10-min flow

  1. 90/90 hip switches — 10/side
  2. Deep squat hold — 60 sec
  3. Pigeon stretch — 30 sec/side
  4. Cat / cow — 10 reps
  5. Thoracic rotations (quadruped) — 10/side
  6. Shoulder dislocates with a band — 10 reps
  7. Wrist circles + wall stretch — 30 sec each direction
  8. Couch stretch (hip flexor) — 30 sec/side
  9. Sleeper stretch (shoulder internal rotation) — 30 sec/side
  10. Standing forward fold — 30 sec

Morning works best — sets your day. Or before bed if mornings are chaos. The list is short on purpose so you'll actually do it.

🚴 3. Cardio (2–3× per week, 30–60 min, off-days)

Goal: Zone-2 aerobic base. Heart rate 60–70% of max — for you (220 − 50 = 170 max) that's 102–119 bpm. You should be able to hold a conversation throughout. If you're gasping, slow down.

Pick one (rotate them)

  • 🚴 Cycling (road or stationary) — joint-friendly, easiest to hold zone 2
  • 🏊 Swimming — best for shoulder mobility too, zero joint loading
  • 🚣 Rowing machine — pulling pattern that translates to BJJ
  • 🚶 Brisk walking — simplest, sustainable, slot it into life
  • 🌀 Elliptical — low-impact backup

Why zone 2 at 50: builds aerobic base = bigger gas tank in rolls + faster recovery between rolls + mitochondrial density that translates into your 60s and 70s. HIIT is fine occasionally but it's not the foundation. Don't run hard intervals when your competing goal is BJJ — they steal recovery from the mat.

🍳 4. Nutrition (for the 5–8kg muscle goal)

Goal: 5–8kg of lean mass over 6–12 months. Realistic ceiling: 0.5–1kg of muscle per month as a natural lifter at 50. Don't rush — slow + sustainable beats sprint + plateau.

The numbers (your bodyweight)

  • Protein: 130–145g/day (1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight). Non-negotiable. This is the lever.
  • Calories: maintenance + 200–300/day surplus. Lean bulk, not bulk. (Maintenance for you ≈ 2,400 kcal — so target 2,600–2,700 on training days.)
  • Carbs: focus around training, not avoided. ~3–4g per kg.

Whole-food anchors

  • Protein: chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, lean beef
  • Carbs: oats, rice, potatoes, sourdough, fruit
  • Fats: olive oil, avocado, nuts, oily fish (omega-3 = anti-inflammatory, big at 50)
  • Veg: leafy greens, berries, peppers — recovery + inflammation control

Easy wins

  • Whey protein shake post-training (25–30g protein)
  • Cottage cheese or Greek yogurt before bed (slow-release casein protein)
  • Add a 4th meal — you're underweight for your height, you're not eating enough
  • Track for 3 days to baseline — most people undershoot protein by 30%
😴 5. Recovery (the silent killer)

At 50, recovery is the gating factor. A 25-year-old can train through bad sleep. You can't. Treat recovery as training, not as the gap between sessions.

  • Sleep: 7–8 hours, prioritised. Screens off 30 min before bed. Cool, dark room.
  • Hydration: pee should be light yellow. Dehydration tanks recovery + grip strength on the mat.
  • Don't roll hard 3 days in a row. Spread sessions: e.g. roll Tue / Thu / Sat, drill or off Mon / Wed / Fri.
  • Tap early. 30 seconds of pride costs 3 weeks of injury. Always tap. Always.
  • Foam roll 5 min after every session — calves, glutes, lats, T-spine. Cheap insurance.
  • Deload week every 4–6 weeks — half volume, no hard rolls. You come back stronger, not weaker.
  • Anti-inflammatory eating — oily fish, leafy greens, berries, turmeric. Reduce alcohol and sugar — both kill recovery at 50.
📒 6. Habit + tracking

Pick ONE technique per week to obsess over. Per Saulo / Roger Gracie philosophy — drill it 100 times before sparring. Don't collect moves; perfect them.

  • Keep a training log — paper notebook or app — every session: what you drilled, what worked, what got submitted on you, who you trained with
  • Review weekly — what's the pattern in your taps? That's your next thing to fix.
  • One specific question per week to your coach — not "show me a move", but "why did X happen in my roll on Tuesday?"
  • Film one roll per month — watch it back. Most painful + most useful learning tool you'll ever use.
  • Stripe-tracking: log when you get each stripe. Most blue belts take 2–3 years. At 50, expect that or a bit longer — you train smarter, not just more.

Weekly schedule that fits this

Mon Strength Day A + 10-min mobility ~50 min total
Tue BJJ + 10-min mobility mat day
Wed Strength Day B + 10-min mobility ~50 min total
Thu BJJ + 10-min mobility mat day
Fri Zone-2 cardio + 10-min mobility ~40 min
Sat BJJ (longest session) + mobility mat day
Sun Full rest or 30-min walk recovery
