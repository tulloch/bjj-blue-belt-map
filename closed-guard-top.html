Closed Guard Top

📷 See this position
Goal here Posture up, strip grips, open their guard, pass. Don't get stuck — the longer you stay, the more chokes and armbars they set up.

If they react…

✊ They control your sleeves / collar
Strip grips + posture
Two-on-one rip down, hands to belt or hips.
▶
Stand to open the guard
Step up, hand on belt + hand on bicep.
▶
📉 They break your posture
Hide the head, return posture
Hide elbows, bury head in chest, walk feet forward, sit up.
▶
Double-shin pass
Crowd them on their back, walk shins up to break the guard.
▶
🔓 They open guard / shift to open guard
Go to Open Guard Top →
Pick a pass for what they switch to (DLR, spider, lasso).
→
Knee cut pass (immediately)
As they open, slice through before they grip up.
▶
Leg drag pass
Drag the top leg across, sit on the hip.
▶
🦵 They pull you into high guard / triangle threat
Posture out + drive head
Stack them, drive head to mat, pull arm out.
▶
Stack pass
Walk feet, drive their knees over their face.
▶

Submissions / threats from here

Closed guard top is a passing position, not a submission position. Stay focused on opening + passing — don't hunt subs from here.

Cross-collar counter
Only if they hand you a clean grip.
