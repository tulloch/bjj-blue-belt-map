Closed Guard Bottom

📷 See this position
Goal here Highest-leverage bottom position. Break their posture, get angles, and chain sweeps + submissions. Your hips are weapons — never lie flat.

If they react…

🧍 They stand up to open the guard
Tripod sweep
Open guard, foot on hip + ankle grip, dump backward.
▶
Sit-up sweep
As they stand, sit up, underhook their leg, take top.
▶
Ankle pick
Same idea — sit up, grab their ankle, push knee.
▶
🧎 They posture up on their knees
Break posture (collar + sleeve pull)
Two grips, hips up + pull down — collapse them onto you.
▶
Hip bump sweep
Sit up, post on far arm, bump them over.
▶
Kimura
If they post a hand on the mat — kimura grip + pivot.
▶
Guillotine
If their head is down — high-elbow guillotine.
▶
✋ They put hands on your chest
Arm drag to back
Pull wrist + tricep across, sit up to the back.
▶
Cross collar choke
Their straight arms = clear path to the collars.
▶
Armbar (straight arm)
Trap one arm, swing leg over the head.
▶
🤝 They get an underhook on one side
Omoplata
Their underhook arm = perfect angle.
▶
Triangle (one arm in, one out)
Their other arm is exposed — cut the angle.
▶
Flower / pendulum sweep
Off the underhook side, swing legs and roll them.
▶
