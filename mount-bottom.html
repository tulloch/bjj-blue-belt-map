Mount Bottom

📷 See this position
Goal here · BLUE-BELT PRIORITY Escape. The worst pin in BJJ. Per Saulo Ribeiro: blue belt = escapes. This is the page you should drill more than any other.

Pick your escape

🌉 Upa (bridge & roll)
🟢 Trap arm + leg, bridge over
Trap one arm and same-side leg, bridge sharp over the trapped shoulder. Stephan Kesting on the 4 most common errors.
▶
🟢 End in Closed Guard Top →
If the upa works, you land in their guard. Now you're passing.
→
🦐 Elbow-knee escape (shrimp out)
🟢 Trap arm, frame, shrimp under
Trap arm with both hands, hip out, knee in. Gordon Ryan demo (technique is universal).
▶
🟢 End in Half Guard Bottom →
First stop is half guard — keep going to closed guard if you can.
→
🦶 Heel drag escape
🟡 Heel drag to half guard
Hook their foot with yours, drag down as you shrimp.
▶

Things they'll try — defend first

⚠️ They go for cross-collar choke
🟢 Strip the first grip, hide the chin
Two-on-one rip on their first hand, chin to chest.
▶
⚠️ They go for armbar / americana
🟢 Elbows in, hands inside
Don't let your arms get isolated — keep them tight.
▶
⚠️ They threaten to take the back
🟢 Don't turn belly-down to escape
Worst mistake. Stay on your back, escape sideways.
▶
