Back Mount

📷 See this position
Goal here Strangle is the priority — not points, not control. Maintain seatbelt + hooks (or body triangle), control the head, hunt the neck. Carlson signature finishing position.

If they react…

✋ They hand-fight your choking arm
🟢 Bow & arrow choke
Lapel grip, far leg trap, fall away. Lachlan Giles walkthrough.
▶
🟢 Arm-trap RNC
Trap their defending arm with your leg, free your choke.
▶
🟡 Switch sides
Move the seatbelt to the other side — they have to defend twice.
▶
↩️ They turn into you (face you)
🟢 Threaten armbar (over-arm)
As they turn, isolate the arm and spin to attack.
▶
🟡 Transition to Mount →
If they fully face you, ride the turn into mount.
→
🦶 They escape one hook (running man / scoot down)
🟡 Re-establish hooks
Hip out, climb the leg back over before they flatten you.
▶
🟡 Switch to body triangle
Lock figure-four around their waist — kills the scoot. Harder for you at 66kg vs heavier opponents.
▶
🟡 Take Mount →
If they're escaping low, climb up to mount as they slip out.
→
🤐 They tuck their chin hard
🟢 Lapel choke (gi)
Their own collar bypasses the chin tuck.
▶
🟢 Mata leon (palm-to-palm)
Push the head forward — tucking helps you, not them.
▶
🟡 Crucifix
Trap one arm with legs, one with arm — opens chokes & armbars. Advanced.
▶

Submissions to hunt anytime

🟢 Rear naked choke
The classic. Stephan Kesting on tightening it properly.
▶
🟢 Bow & arrow choke
Highest % gi back attack. Lachlan Giles.
▶

Palm-to-palm version of RNC.
▶
