Open Guard Top (Passing)

📷 See this position
Goal here Read their guard, pick the right pass. Don't pass through their grips — strip first or pass around them. Pressure or speed, pick a lane.

What guard are they playing?

🦵 De La Riva (DLR)
Leg drag pass
Highest % vs DLR. Strip the hook, drag across.
▶
Knee cut
Kill the DLR hook, cut your far knee through.

🕷️ Spider Guard
Strip sleeve grips + push knees down
Two-on-one rip down, drive knees to mat.
▶
X-pass / leg-weave
Throw the leg over, walk around to side.
▶
Smash / over-under pass
Pressure pass — collapse spider guard with body weight.

🪢 Lasso
Pummel out / spin out
Step over the lasso leg, spin to the open side.
▶
Knee cut + smash
Crush the lasso to the floor, slice through.

🦋 Butterfly
Over-under pass
