Half Guard Bottom

📷 See this position
Goal here Get on your side, get the underhook, sweep to top. Avoid deep half / lockdown — joint-heavy and bad for your age. Old-school sweep is your friend.

If they react…

🤝 You have the underhook
🟢 Old school sweep
Underhook + far ankle, dump them backward. Fundamental.
▶
🟡 Plan B sweep
Stand them up, sweep to top.
▶
🟢 Back take
Climb the underhook side, take the back.
▶
🦾 They whizzer / kill the underhook
⚪ Switch to deep half
Dive under the leg. Skip at 50 — joint-heavy, scramble-heavy.
▶
⚪ Waiter sweep
From deep half — bench-press their leg up.
▶
🟡 John Wayne sweep
Bicep grip + drive shoulder, roll them.
▶
🥞 They flatten you out
🟢 Don't! Get to your side first
Frame, hip escape, restore the underhook.
▶
🟢 Insert knee shield
Knee across hip, frame on bicep, create distance.
▶
🤲 They cross-face hard
🟢 Push the head, get under-hook back
Frame on jaw, swim arm under.
▶
🟢 Z-guard / knee shield
Insert the knee, kill the cross-face pressure.
▶

Submissions to hunt anytime

🟢 Kimura
If they post on far side.
▶
🟡 Guillotine
If their head is low + your underhook is in.
▶
🟡 Straight ankle lock
From z-guard / lockdown, attack the foot.
▶
🟡 Triangle
If they post the far arm too high.
