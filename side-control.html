Side Control

📷 See this position
Goal here Hold them flat, stay tight to the shoulder, isolate the far arm. Kimura grip is your green threat — sets up subs, transitions, and back-takes.

If they react…

🛡️ They frame on your neck or hip
🟢 Switch to North-South →
Spin to head — kills the frame, opens kimura/north-south choke.
→
🟢 Knee on Belly →
Step over their frame, drive knee in.
→
🟢 Kimura the framing arm
Their elbow is up — perfect kimura grip target. John Danaher's perfect kimura.
▶
🐢 They turn away (toward turtle)
🟢 Take the Back →
Slide hooks in as they expose. Don't let them re-flatten.
→
🟡 Clock choke (gi)
Far collar grip, walk around the head.
▶
🦐 They shrimp toward you
🟡 Climb to Mount →
Step over their hip as they create space.
→
🟡 Re-pin with cross-face
Drive shoulder into the jaw, kill the shrimp.
▶
🤝 They get the underhook
🟢 Kimura the underhooking arm
They handed you the grip — finish or use it to switch.
▶
🟡 Sprawl + whizzer counter
Don't let them bridge into half guard.
▶
